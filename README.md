# 🤖 AI & Data Science Job Market Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes AI and Data Science job-market data to understand
job demand, salaries, experience levels, remote work, locations, and
other factors related to different job roles.

I used Python and different data analysis libraries to clean the data,
explore it, create visualizations, and find useful patterns from the
dataset.


##  Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the most common AI and Data Science job roles.
- Compare salaries across different job roles.
- Analyze salary differences across experience levels.
- Identify high-demand and high-paying roles.
- Analyze remote-work opportunities.
- Compare job opportunities across locations.
- Study relationships between numerical variables.
- Extract useful insights from the dataset.

##  Dataset

The dataset contains information about AI and Data Science jobs,
including job roles, salary, experience level, company information,
location, remote work, and other career-related attributes.

The dataset was used to study different patterns in the AI and Data
Science job market.
### Dataset Source

[https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uditjain13/ai-and-data-science-job-salaries-2026]


##  Data Cleaning

The following data-cleaning steps were performed:

- Checked the dataset structure.
- Checked for missing values.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Checked data types.
- Examined numerical and categorical columns.
- Handled data issues where required.
- Prepared the data for analysis and visualization.

##  Exploratory Data Analysis

The project explores different aspects of the job market, including:

- Job role distribution
- Experience-level distribution
- Employment type
- Company size
- Company location
- Remote-work distribution
- Salary distribution
- Salary by job role
- Salary by experience level

##  Key Findings

- Data Scientist, Data Engineer, Data Analyst and other related roles
  are among the frequently occurring job roles in the dataset.

- Years of experience has a positive relationship with salary.

- Years of experience has a relatively strong positive relationship
  with bonus percentage.

- Salary and fear of AI automation show a weak negative relationship.

- Job demand and salary are different across different roles.

- Remote-work opportunities vary across job roles and experience levels.
